## Mint Your First Phygital NFT

Get started with EtchNFT in just a few simple steps. This guide will walk you through minting your first phygital NFT using our Farcaster mini app.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- ✅ **Farcaster account** — [Sign up at Warpcast](https://warpcast.com)
- ✅ **Crypto wallet** — Ethereum or Solana wallet with some funds for gas fees
- ✅ **Verified address** — Link your wallet to your Farcaster profile

:::tip
New to crypto wallets? We recommend [MetaMask](https://metamask.io) for Ethereum or [Phantom](https://phantom.app) for Solana.
:::

## Step 1: Access the Mini App

There are two ways to access the EtchNFT mini app:

### Option A: Direct Link
Visit [etchnft.com/miniapp](https://etchnft.com/miniapp) directly in your browser.

### Option B: Via Farcaster
1. Open Warpcast or your preferred Farcaster client
2. Look for EtchNFT posts with embedded mini apps
3. Tap to open the interactive experience

## Step 2: Connect Your Wallet

The mini app will automatically detect and connect to your verified Farcaster wallet:

1. **Ethereum users**: Your verified ETH address will be detected
2. **Solana users**: Your verified SOL address will be connected  
3. **Multi-chain users**: Choose your preferred chain using the toggle

:::note
Wallet connection is seamless — no popups or manual connection required!
:::

## Step 3: Choose Your Chain

Select your preferred blockchain:

- **🔷 Ethereum** — Premium collectibles, established ecosystem
- **🟣 Solana** — Fast transactions, low fees
- **🔵 Base** — Layer 2 Ethereum with reduced costs

Each chain shows:
- Current wallet balance
- Estimated transaction fees
- Processing time

## Step 4: Select Your Collectible

Choose from three collectible types:

### 🏆 Etched Plaque
**Best for**: Awards, achievements, commemoratives
- **Material**: Premium metal (aluminum/brass/steel)
- **Size**: 4" x 6" standard, custom sizes available
- **Customization**: Text, logos, QR codes
- **Price**: Starting at $49 + gas fees

### 👕 Custom Tee
**Best for**: Apparel, limited drops, community items
- **Material**: 100% organic cotton
- **Sizes**: XS-3XL available
- **Printing**: High-quality screen printing
- **Price**: Starting at $29 + gas fees

### 💎 Acrylic Relic
**Best for**: Art pieces, photo preservation, displays
- **Material**: UV-resistant acrylic
- **Size**: 6" x 8" standard, custom cuts available
- **Features**: Optional LED backlighting
- **Price**: Starting at $39 + gas fees

## Step 5: Customize Your Design

After selecting your collectible type:

1. **Upload artwork** — PNG, JPG, or SVG files
2. **Add text** — Custom messages, names, dates
3. **Choose colors** — Material and print colors
4. **Preview design** — See exactly how it will look

:::tip
For best results, use high-resolution images (300 DPI minimum) and vector graphics when possible.
:::

## Step 6: Review & Mint

Before finalizing:

1. **Review details** — Double-check design and specifications
2. **Gas estimation** — See total cost including blockchain fees
3. **Shipping info** — Add delivery address for physical item
4. **Terms acceptance** — Agree to terms and conditions

## Step 7: Complete Transaction

1. **Confirm mint** — Tap the mint button
2. **Wallet approval** — Approve the blockchain transaction
3. **Processing** — Wait for confirmation (usually 1-3 minutes)
4. **Success!** — Your NFT is minted and order is placed

## Step 8: Share Your Creation

After successful minting:

1. **Auto-compose** — Mini app opens share dialog
2. **Custom message** — Add personal message to the post
3. **Chain attribution** — Shows which blockchain you used
4. **Transaction link** — Includes link to view on block explorer

Example share message:
```
Just minted my custom plaque NFT on @etchnft! 🏆✨

⟠ Minted on Ethereum
💝 Phygital collectible - own it physically AND digitally.

Tx: 0x1234...5678
```

## Troubleshooting

### Wallet Connection Issues
- Ensure your wallet is properly connected to Farcaster
- Try refreshing the mini app
- Check that you have sufficient funds for gas fees

### Transaction Failures
- Increase gas limit if transaction fails
- Check network congestion and try again
- Ensure wallet has enough funds for fees

### Design Problems
- Use high-resolution images (300 DPI minimum)  
- Ensure text is readable at final size
- Check file format compatibility (PNG, JPG, SVG)
